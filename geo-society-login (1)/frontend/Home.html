Dr. Sarah Geology Discussion

2 hours ago • Public

Just returned from fieldwork in the Shire Valley. The sediment layers tell a fascinating story of climatic changes over the past 10,000 years. Found evidence of alternating wet and dry periods that correlate with migration patterns in the region.

The most interesting discovery was a layer of volcanic ash from the 19th century that perfectly preserved pollen samples, giving us unprecedented insight into the vegetation at that time. This could revolutionize our understanding of pre-colonial agricultural practices in Malawi.
